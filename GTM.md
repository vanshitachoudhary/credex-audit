# GTM Strategy — AI Spend Audit Tool

## 🎯 Overview

AI Spend Audit Tool is a free utility that helps startups and developers identify overspending across AI tools and convert those insights into actionable cost savings.

It is designed as a **viral audit + lead generation tool** for Credex.

---

## 👤 Target Users

Primary users:

- Startup Founders (Seed → Series A)
- Engineering Managers (10–200 employees)
- Tech Leads managing AI tool stacks
- Indie Hackers using multiple SaaS AI tools

---

## 🔥 Core Insight

Users do NOT actively think about AI overspending.

They only care when:
- Monthly SaaS bill increases unexpectedly
- They scale team and add tool seats
- They prepare for fundraising and cost audit
- They see benchmark comparisons

👉 This tool creates that “oh shit we are overpaying” moment.

## 🚀 0 → 100 Users Strategy (No Budget)

### Phase 1: Manual Outreach (Day 1–5)
- DM founders on X / LinkedIn
- Offer free AI spend audit
- Target 20–30 founders per day

Goal:
👉 30–50 manual audits completed

---

### Phase 2: Content Loop (Day 3–10)
Post real audit insights:

Examples:
- “This 12-person startup is wasting $4,800/year on AI tools”
- “Most teams overpay for ChatGPT + Cursor combos”

Include:
- screenshots (anonymized)
- savings numbers
- tool link

Goal:
👉 viral engagement → organic traffic

---

### Phase 3: Community Distribution (Day 5–14)
Post in:
- Indie Hackers
- Reddit SaaS threads
- YC Startup forums

Positioning:
👉 “Free AI Spend Optimization Tool”

---

### Phase 4: Product-Led Viral Loop
Each audit generates:
- shareable public URL
- savings breakdown
- strong emotional trigger (money saved)

Users naturally share within teams.

---

## 🧲 Growth Loop

1. User runs audit
2. Sees savings ($$$)
3. Shares result link
4. Another founder clicks & tries
5. Funnel repeats
